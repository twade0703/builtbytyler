Where it comes together

The best part is the combination.

A drone or a robot is never just mechanical or just software. It is both, designed and integrated as one system. Engineering the structure, writing the control that brings it to life, then watching the two click together into something that actually flies or drives — that intersection is where the interesting problems live, and it is what I chase with every build.

It is also why the software side of this studio exists. Most people building websites have never had to make anything work under load. I have, and it changes how you build software: you test it, you document it, and you hand it over so somebody else can maintain it.

  • Everything ships with its source CAD, firmware, code and documentation. If you paid for it, you own it outright and you can take it anywhere.
  • Nothing is bought and rebadged No white-labelled hardware, no page-builder templates. It is designed here or it is not sold here.
  • You deal with me No account manager, no handoff to a junior. The person who scopes the work is the person who builds it.
  • I will talk you out of it If a build or a piece of software is the wrong answer for what you are trying to do, I would rather say so on the first call.
