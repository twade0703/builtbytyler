About it

  • Bright white LED switched by a logic-level MOSFET. PWM the IN pin to dim it.
  • MOSFET driven: the GPIO only drives a gate, so 3.3 V Pi/ESP32 pins work
  • Honest rating: ~350 mA / ~1 W into the LED so it survives on a small FR4 board
  • Thermal pad soldered to the GND pour
