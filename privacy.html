Privacy

Privacy policy

BuiltByTyler LLC is a small studio in Monterey, California. This page is the whole of what we collect, where it comes from, who handles it and how long we keep it. It covers this website, the demo sites we build at see.builtbytyler.com, and the calls and texts our sales team makes from BuiltByTyler phone lines.

The short version

  • We never sell personal information, and we never share it for advertising.
  • We do not share your mobile number or your text-message consent with anyone for marketing or promotional purposes.
  • Our sales calls are not recorded, transcribed or summarised by AI.
  • We text you only if you say yes on a call. Reply STOP and we never text or call that number again.
  • This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising pixels.

Visiting builtbytyler.com

  • When you email us — your name, your email address, and whatever you write.
  • When you use an order or waitlist link — it opens your own email program or hands you to Stripe. There is no database behind it.
  • When you apply to sell for us — the answers and the voice memo you send from our application page. See below.
  • When you pay — Stripe collects your name, email and billing address on its own checkout page and shows them to us afterwards. Card details are never entered on, stored on, or passed through builtbytyler.com.

No accounts, no logins, no analytics, no advertising pixels, and no cookies set by this site. The demo pages at see.builtbytyler.com work the same way: no forms, no cookies, no tracking.

If we have contacted your business

Where your business details come from. We find local businesses through their public listings, mostly Google Business Profiles. From the listing we keep the business name, phone number, city and address, category, opening hours, star rating and number of reviews, any website it links to, and the public reviews and photos on it. We do not buy contact lists, and we contact businesses, not private individuals.

The demo site. We may build a sample website for your business from those public details, to show you what a site could look like. Every demo says at the top that we built it unasked and that it is not your website, is hidden from search engines, and costs you nothing. If you would like it taken down, tell us on a call, by text or by email and it comes down; we will not contact you again.

Calls. A member of our sales team may call your business from a BuiltByTyler phone line. Calls are not recorded, not transcribed and not summarised by AI. We keep a short log of each call: the number called, the date and time, how long it lasted, how it went, and any note the rep makes (for example, “call back Thursday”). We call a number at most once a day and three times in all, between 9 am and 8 pm your time, Monday to Saturday.

Voicemail. If you call one of our lines and leave a voicemail, it is recorded so we can listen to it and call you back. Nobody outside BuiltByTyler listens to it.

Text messages. On a call, our rep will usually offer to text you a link — to the demo site we built for your business, to a demo of our AI Front Desk with a number you can call to hear it answer, or to the page where you can pay for a website or for AI Front Desk. We send it only if you say yes on the call, and the rep notes your answer before anything goes out. Each text says who it is from and ends with “Reply STOP to opt out.” Expect one or two messages per conversation, plus replies to anything you text us. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, or STOP to opt out at any time. The exact words our reps use to ask, and how your yes is recorded: builtbytyler.com/text-consent.

Asking us to stop. Tell the rep on the call, reply STOP to any text, or email twade@builtbytyler.com. Your number goes on our company-wide do-not-call list the same day, and nobody at BuiltByTyler calls or texts it again. We keep that list so the promise holds, which is why your number stays on it.

Your mobile number and text-message consent

We do not share, sell, rent or provide your mobile phone number, or your text-message opt-in data and consent, to any third party or affiliate for marketing or promotional purposes. The only outside companies that handle them are the service providers that carry our calls and texts and run our tools, and each does so only to provide its service to us. Your consent covers messages from BuiltByTyler about the product you asked about on the call (your demo and website, or AI Front Desk), and nothing else.

If you buy a website from us

Stripe takes the payment and shows us your name, email and billing address. To build and run your site we use the details, photos and wording you send us, along with the public listing details above. Your site's hosting and domain records hold what any website needs to run.

Who else handles it

We use a few service providers: for email, payments, website hosting, our phone lines, and the tools we build demo sites with. They handle information only to do that work for us. We may also share information if the law requires it, for example to answer a valid legal request. We do not share personal information with anyone for their own marketing.

Our sales reps

Our reps are independent contractors who sign an agreement that keeps our lists confidential. They work only inside our own sales app, see only the businesses assigned to them, and may not copy your details anywhere else. The app at sales.builtbytyler.com is for our reps only; it uses a sign-in cookie so a rep stays signed in. It is not a public site.

If you apply to sell for us

What we collect. Your answers on the application page (your name, email, mobile, state, sales experience and the other questions on it) and the voice memo you record or upload. We do not keep your IP address or anything about your device. To stop the form being flooded, we keep a scrambled, one-way code made from your IP address for one day; it cannot be turned back into the address.

What we use it for. Only to decide on your application and to contact you about it. Tyler Wade listens to every voice memo and makes every decision himself. No automated system accepts or rejects anyone, and we never share applications with other companies.

Where it is kept. In storage at Cloudflare, which hosts this website, then on BuiltByTyler's own computer, with a copy emailed to Tyler's business inbox (Google Workspace) so he sees it the same day. We never sell it or share it with anyone.

How long. If we don't end up working together, we delete your application and voice memo within 12 months. If we do, it becomes part of your contractor file. Email twade@builtbytyler.com at any time to have it deleted sooner.

How long we keep it

  • Emails and ordersas long as the job needs, and seven years for anything with money attached (tax records)
  • Business details from public listingswhile we may contact you; deleted when you ask, except the do-not-call entry
  • Call logsat least three years
  • Records of your yes to a textat least four years
  • The do-not-call listat least ten years, so we never call you again
  • Voicemailsuntil we have dealt with the message
  • Job applications and voice memosup to 12 months if we don't work together, then deleted

Seeing it, changing it, deleting it

Email twade@builtbytyler.com and ask. We will tell you what we hold within a week, correct anything wrong, and delete whatever we are not required to keep. No form and no fee. If you ask us to delete your business's details, we keep only your phone number on the do-not-call list, so we never contact you again.

California. We do not sell or share personal information as California law defines those words, and we do not disclose personal information to third parties for their direct marketing. Anyone may make the requests above, wherever they live.

Keeping it safe

Our sales records live in our own app and with the providers above. Each rep signs in with a personal access code that we can switch off at once, and reps must tell us the same day if a device with our app on it is lost. No system is perfectly secure, and if something went wrong that affected you, we would tell you.

Do Not Track

This site does not follow you to other websites, so there is nothing for a Do Not Track signal to switch off, and the site does not respond to those signals. No third party collects information about what you do across other websites through this one.

Changes to this policy

If it changes in a way that matters, the new version goes up here with a new date below, and anyone with an open order gets an email.
