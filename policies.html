Before you order

Lead times, shipping & refunds

Everything here is built by hand, one at a time. That changes how ordering works compared with buying something off a shelf, so this page says exactly what to expect.

Everything is made to order

Nothing sits in a warehouse. When you order, the build starts. Current lead times, counted from the day payment clears:

    If a build is going to run past its stated lead time, you get an email before the date passes, not after. If a delay isn't acceptable to you at that point, you can cancel for a full refund.

    Shipping

    • Free shipping within the continental United States.
    • Shipped insured with tracking. The tracking number is emailed the day it goes out.
    • Alaska, Hawaii and international orders are quoted separately — email before ordering.
    • Risk of loss passes on delivery. If a carrier loses or damages a package, that's mine to resolve, not yours.

    Changing or cancelling an order

    • Before the build starts — cancel for any reason, full refund, no questions.
    • Once the build has started — I refund what I haven't already spent. Materials and parts bought specifically for your order are deducted, and I'll show you the receipts.
    • Once it has shipped — the returns section below applies.

    To cancel or change anything, email twade@builtbytyler.com with your order number.

    If something arrives damaged or faulty

    Tell me within 14 days of delivery and I'll repair it, replace it, or refund it — your choice. Photographs help but aren't a condition. Shipping both ways is on me.

    This covers workmanship and shipping damage. It doesn't cover crash damage, water damage, or parts worn out in normal use — these are flying and moving machines, and they do get broken. I'll always quote a repair.

    Returns on made-to-order builds

    Because every build is made specifically for you, finished builds are not returnable for change of mind. Under California law, custom-ordered goods are exempt from the general refund requirements (California Attorney General — Refund Policies).

    That exemption is not a shield for bad work. If a build isn't right, the faulty-goods section above applies and I would rather fix it than argue about it.

    Payment

    Payment is taken by Stripe on their own secure checkout page. Card details are never entered on, stored on, or transmitted through builtbytyler.com. Your statement will read BUILTBYTYLER.

    Custom engineering work

    Custom projects are quoted individually and run on a written scope agreed before any money changes hands. Those terms sit in the quote and override this page where they differ.
