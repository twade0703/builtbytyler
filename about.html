The short version

I have been taking things apart and putting them back together wrong, then right, for as long as I can remember. Everything here started that way.

The mechanical side came first. Parametric CAD, fatigue and flow simulation, prototyping and fabrication — parts analysed before they get cut and tested after they are assembled, iterated until they hold up under load rather than on a screen. A rover chassis that keeps six wheels on the ground over rough terrain, a tilt-rotor that has to survive the transition from hovering to flying, a laser mount that has to hold a beam on something moving. Every one of those is a problem that does not care how good the render looked.

The software side grew out of it, because a drone or a robot is never just mechanical. It is both, designed and integrated as one system: engineering the structure, writing the control that brings it to life, then watching the two click together into something that actually flies or drives. That intersection is where the interesting problems live, and it is what I chase with every build.

It is also why I build software for other businesses. Most people making websites have never had to make something work under load, where a failure is a part on the floor rather than a bug report. That changes how you build: you test it, you document it, and you hand it over so somebody else can maintain it. A business website is a small problem by comparison — which is exactly why it should be done properly, quickly, and for a price you can see before you call.

I work out of Monterey, California. If you email me, I am the one who answers.

How I work

Four things I will not trade away.

Every one of these costs me something. They are here because the alternative costs you more, and because a promise you can check is worth more than a page of adjectives.

  • Everything ships with its source CAD, firmware, code and documentation. If you paid for it, you own it outright and you can take it anywhere.
  • Nothing is bought and rebadged No white-labelled hardware, no page-builder templates. It is designed here or it is not sold here.
  • You deal with me No account manager, no handoff to a junior. The person who scopes the work is the person who builds it, and the person who answers the email.
  • I will talk you out of it If a build or a piece of software is the wrong answer for what you are trying to do, I would rather say so on the first call than take the money.
