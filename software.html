Straight answers

FAQ

Do I own the site, or am I renting it?

You own it. The domain is registered in your name and the code is yours. I host it and pay for the hosting and the domain myself, so there is nothing else for you to set up or pay for.

Why is there a monthly fee at all?

Because a site that is never touched again quietly rots: dependencies go out of date, security headers drift, forms break when a provider changes an API, and content goes stale. The plan covers hosting, the domain, backups, updates and small changes. You tell me it is down and I am on it right away.

Two or three days? Really?

Yes. There is no queue and nobody to hand it to, so it gets built as soon as we have talked. Bigger builds, like a booking system or a portal, are scoped on their own and I will tell you the timeline before you pay anything.

Can you work with the site I already have?

Sometimes. If it is on a platform that lets me at the code, yes. If it is locked inside a builder that will not export, rebuilding is usually cheaper than fighting it, and I will tell you which of the two you are looking at on the first call.

How do you handle payments and customer data?

Payments run through Stripe on their own hosted checkout — card details are never entered on, stored on, or transmitted through a site I build. Customer data stays in your accounts, under your ownership, with access I can be removed from.

What do you need from me to start?

Send me an email or book a call. I call you to go over what you want built, the style and the format.
