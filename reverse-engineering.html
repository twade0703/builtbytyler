CombineCAD

A 3D modelling program I built with Claude inside it. You describe the part in plain words. The AI builds it one feature at a time and checks it against the real parts it has to fit.

TypedBuild a 1/10 scale RC car chassis. A 3 mm plate, 300 by 110 mm with rounded ends, a 6 mm lip and lightening pockets down the middle. Wheelbase is 260 mm.

TypedDesign a model rocket around an Estes BT-50 body tube and a D12 motor. A 400 mm tube, an ogive nose cone with a shoulder, and a motor mount.

TypedStart a 5 inch FPV freestyle quad frame. A 4 mm bottom plate, 30.5 mm stack holes, four arms in a true X for a 225 mm wheelbase, sized for 2306 motors.

ThenThe RC car taken all the way, with Claude writing the build.

01 / 05Claude sketches the plate on the top plane and extrudes it 3 mm.

02 / 05It adds the lip, cuts one pocket and patterns it down the middle, then measures the plate against the spec.

03 / 05Next message asks for a 540 motor mount. It takes the hole pattern from its hardware catalog, slots the holes for gear mesh and braces the plate with two ribs.

04 / 05Four wheels, revolved as separate bodies at the 260 mm wheelbase, with hex hubs and bearing seats.

05 / 05A steering servo mount goes on the front. The check: five closed solids, nothing intersecting, all four wheels the same volume to 0.01 mm³.

01 / 04Claude revolves the 400 mm body tube from the BT-50's real sizes.

02 / 04It turns down the motor mount tube. A D12 is 24.0 mm and a BT-50 is 24.1 mm inside, so a second tube cannot fit. The motor slides straight into the body, the way Estes builds them.

03 / 04Four fins patterned round the tail and filleted at the root, plus a launch lug.

04 / 04Then it works out the balance itself. 0.84 calibres bare, short of the 1.0 rule. 1.69 with a parachute and shock cord in, which passes.

How it works

  1. Take it apart

    An AI agent and a disassembler go through the compiled program, the .exe, its DLLs and its data files, the way an engineer goes through a teardown. The machine code is decompiled back into readable source, functions get real names, and the agent maps what loads, what each system owns and where a change can be made safely. Every finding is checked against the program running, not guessed from the code alone.

    REA/Ghidra/Claude Code/Runtime tracing

  2. Build it again

    Once both programs are understood, they can be combined: one app's features rebuilt inside another, as a mod, an injected DLL or a whole engine. What ships is only what I wrote.

    Rust/C#/Lua/Unreal/Blender

  3. Prove it

    Every project is stamped Playable, Partial or Cap, and an automated test drives the real build before a stamp goes on. If part of it does not work yet, the stamp says so.

    Smoke tests/Scripted input/Screenshots

04 — The rules

04

Offline, our own code, nothing cracked.

Reverse engineering is for learning how something works and building on it, not for breaking it. These rules hold on everything I publish or release.

  • 01 Offline only No online or multiplayer games, and anti-cheat is never touched.
  • 02 No cracks, no DRM No keys, no cheats, no paid product unlocked.
  • 03 Our code ships Decompiled code is never published, and downloads carry no ripped files.
  • 04 EULA first Each target's licence is read before work starts.
