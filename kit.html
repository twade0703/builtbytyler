Hardware kit — 38 modules

The sensor kit, redrawn.

Thirty-eight sensor and output modules, every one designed from scratch on a black board with the same pinout. They run on 3.3 V or 5 V, so one kit works with an ESP32, a Raspberry Pi or an Arduino. Scan the code on the back of any board for its wiring guide and demo.

Every board, the same rules

Plug in any module the same way.

The usual cheap kits mix pin orders, assume 5 V and drive buzzers straight off a GPIO pin. These do not.

  • 01One pinoutGND is always pin 1 on the left, then power, then signals. Names are printed on both sides.
  • 023.3 V or 5 VEvery board runs on either. The 5 V-only parts (gas heaters, relay) still give 3.3 V-safe signals.
  • 03Driven properlyBuzzers, relay, laser and IR run through a transistor that stays off while your board boots.
  • 04Scan for the guideA QR code on the back of every board opens its page: wiring for ESP32, Uno and Pi, a demo and the code.
